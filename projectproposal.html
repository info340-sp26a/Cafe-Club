Primary Users of this Web App

For the users of this web application, our team believes it will primarily be used by: cafe owners, managers, cafe staff, small business owners, culinary students, event planners/pop-up vendors

  • Cafe Owners & Managers - We believe these are going to be our primary users who would use our web application in order to design, update, and also finalize the menus for their own business.
  • Cafe Staff- By providing the cafe staff with this web application, we aim to help them view and help refine the cafe’s menu structure, item description, and also pricing whenever needed. This also allows them to be continuously updated on any menu updates and are able to keep them knowledgeable about the cafe’s menu.
  • Small Business Entrepreneurs - Those who are planning to open a cafe and want to start experimenting with various menu ideas
  • Culinary Students- The web can be used for students who are often practicing menu design or creating mock concepts for learning or fun purposes
  • Event Planners or Pop-up Vendors - We believe this group of people are often redesigning or creating new menus whenever they are assigned to a new event. With this app we are able to allow them to save their menus and have temporary menus for various events
